Nashik
Revealed

India's wine capital and a sacred pilgrimage city in one — where the holy Godavari flows past ancient ghats, and vineyards stretch across sun-drenched valleys.

🍷 Wine Country 🛕 Sacred Ghats ⛰️ Sahyadri Trails 🌿 Sula Vineyards 🪔 Kumbh Mela

Must Visit

Nashik's iconic landmarks

🛕

Trimbakeshwar Temple

One of the twelve sacred Jyotirlingas, this ancient Shiva temple at the source of the Godavari river draws pilgrims from across India.

Explore →
🍷

Sula Vineyards

India's most celebrated winery — set among rolling hills, offering tastings, tours, a lakeside resort, and the annual SulaFest music festival.

Explore →
🏛️

Pandavleni Caves

Twenty-four ancient Buddhist rock-cut caves dating from the 3rd century BCE, carved into the Trirashmi Hills overlooking the city.

Explore →
🪔

Ramkund Ghat

The holiest bathing ghat on the Godavari river — believed to be where Lord Rama bathed during his exile, and a deeply spiritual place at dawn.

Explore →
⛰️

Saptashringi Devi Temple

A dramatic hilltop temple 65 km from Nashik, carved into a cliff face at 4659 feet — one of Maharashtra's most revered goddess shrines.

Explore →
🏔️

Kalsubai Peak

At 1646 metres, Kalsubai is the highest peak in Maharashtra — a challenging and rewarding trek through tribal villages and Sahyadri forest.

Explore →

Wine & Food

Taste Nashik

Sula Sauvignon Blanc

Nashik's flagship wine — crisp, fruity, and perfect with the region's mild climate. Available at every winery and across India.

Misal Pav

Nashik's version of this Maharashtrian classic is spicier and heartier — the Kolhapuri-inspired gravy is unforgettable.

Saoji Chicken

A fiery Vidarbha-style dry chicken curry that Nashik has adopted as a beloved local specialty — not for the faint-hearted.

Grape Products

Fresh grapes, grape juice, raisins, and grape jam straight from local farms — Nashik is India's largest grape-producing region.
