Food Culture

Taste Pune

Misal Pav

The quintessential Pune breakfast — spicy sprouted curry topped with farsan, onions, and served with buttered pav.

Pav Bhaji

FC Road's legendary street stalls serve this buttery vegetable mash with a side of Pune nostalgia.

Mastani

Pune's thick, royal ice cream milkshake topped with kulfi and dry fruits — a drink that became a cultural icon.

Shrewsbury Biscuits

Kayani Bakery's legendary buttery biscuits — a colonial-era tradition that Punekars swear by, exported worldwide.
