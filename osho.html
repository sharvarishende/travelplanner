Osho Ashram

A Sanctuary for Inner Journeys

About this Place

Why visit Osho

The Osho International Meditation Resort in Koregaon Park is one of the most famous spiritual destinations in the world, welcoming over 200,000 visitors annually from more than 100 countries. Founded by the spiritual teacher Osho (Bhagwan Shree Rajneesh), the resort is a lush 32-acre urban oasis of bamboo groves, marble paths, and serene meditation halls. Visitors come to participate in a diverse range of meditation programs — from active dynamic meditation at dawn to silent evening meetings — and to simply experience the rare quality of stillness at the heart of a busy Indian city.

History & Heritage

The story behind it

Osho moved to Pune in 1974 and established his ashram here, attracting thousands of Western seekers throughout the 1970s and 80s. After Osho's passing in 1990, the ashram was transformed into the Osho International Meditation Resort. The Osho Auditorium, a stunning pyramid-shaped hall built in black marble, is the centrepiece of the resort and hosts the daily evening White Robe Brotherhood meditation.

Visitor Tips

Before you go

01

You must purchase a resort pass (daily or multi-day) to enter — book in advance online.

02

The famous Dynamic Meditation begins at 6 AM — arrive early.

03

Wear robes inside the meditation halls — maroon during the day, white in the evening.

04

The resort's café serves excellent food; vegetarian and international cuisine available.

05

A Pune Health Certificate (HIV test result) may be required for longer stays — check the website.
