About Maharashtra

A state of
endless wonder

Maharashtra is India's second-largest state — a land that gracefully holds Bollywood glamour, ancient cave temples carved two millennia ago, and the lush peaks of the Sahyadri ranges, all at once.

Whether you're drawn to the chaotic beauty of Mumbai's Marine Drive at dusk, the echo of Peshwa history in Pune's Shaniwarwada, or the quiet mist over Mahabaleshwar's valleys — Maharashtra never lets a traveller leave unchanged.

Why Maharashtra

What makes it unforgettable

🏰

Forts & Maratha Legacy

From Raigad — the capital of Shivaji Maharaj's empire — to the sea fort of Murud-Janjira, Maharashtra's forts are living museums of one of history's greatest military empires.

🕌

UNESCO Cave Temples

Ajanta's painted caves and Ellora's rock-cut monasteries represent one of humanity's greatest artistic achievements — carved by hand over centuries and still breathtaking today.

🍛

A Culinary Universe

Vada Pav on the streets, Kolhapuri rassa in the heartland, Malvani seafood on the coast — Maharashtra's food is as layered and complex as its history.

🌿

Nature & Hill Stations

Mahabaleshwar, Lonavala, Matheran — the Sahyadri ranges offer misty escapes just hours from any city, bursting green in the monsoon and pleasantly cool through winter.

🎬

Bollywood & Culture

Mumbai is the world's largest film industry by output. From studio tours to glittering multiplexes — cinema is woven into the city's very fabric.
