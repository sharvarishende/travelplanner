Explore by Neighbourhood

Every street has its own soul

Bandra

Trendy · Creative · Coastal

Bollywood's backyard. Street art, sea-facing cafés, Bandstand promenade, and a creative energy that is unmistakably Mumbai.

Fort & CST

Colonial · Heritage · Business

Victorian Gothic architecture lines these streets — CST station, the High Court, and Bombay University form a UNESCO Heritage precinct.

Juhu

Beach · Street Food · Film

Juhu Beach at sunset with bhel puri in hand is a quintessential Mumbai experience. The street food here is legendary.

Lower Parel

Urban · Mills · Restaurants

Old textile mills reborn as galleries, co-working spaces, and fine dining. Mumbai's most dramatic urban transformation.

Chor Bazaar

Antiques · History · Markets

The "Thieves' Market" of Mumbai — a labyrinth of antique furniture, vintage Bollywood posters, and forgotten treasures.

Worli

Modern · Sea Link · Art

Home to the iconic Bandra-Worli Sea Link, Worli Sea Face, and galleries of contemporary Indian art.

Street Food Culture

Taste Mumbai

Vada Pav

Mumbai's heartbeat. A spiced potato fritter in a soft roll — ₹15 and absolutely unstoppable.

Pav Bhaji

Juhu Beach's crowning glory — buttery vegetable mash with fresh bread, topped with lime and raw onion.

Bombay Sandwich

Chutney, cucumber, boiled potato, tomato, beetroot — all pressed and grilled into an absurdly satisfying snack.

Bhel Puri

Puffed rice, sev, tamarind chutney, and raw mango — best eaten by the sea as the wind steals half of it.
